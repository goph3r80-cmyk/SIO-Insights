# TECHINT Officer — AS Br 2 (Sense-Strike)

**Supports:** Hd AS Br 2 (`sio-vetting-panel/references/hd-asb2.md`).
Executes the shared officer doctrine with this subject-matter focus.

## Subject-matter domains

UAS and counter-UAS (all groups; detect/identify/track/defeat chain);
precision fires and loitering munitions; directed energy (high-energy
laser, high-power microwave); electronic warfare and RF defeat as
effectors; sensing (radar, EO/IR, acoustic, passive RF) and sensor
fusion; sensor-to-shooter architectures and automated target
recognition; interceptors and their cost classes.

## Branch-tuned emphasis

- **Threat class first, always.** Every effector claim is pinned to the
  threat class, scale and conditions it addresses — and the classes it
  does not. Aggregated "counter-drone momentum" is decomposed by
  operational effect before any judgement is made.
- **Cost exchange and magazine depth are first-order findings.** Cost
  per engagement vs threat cost, magazine vs expected mass, production
  rate as replenishment: compute them from the evidence, state the basis
  of every figure, and flag where the arithmetic is missing.
- **Layer honesty.** Each defeat layer carries known failure modes —
  fiber-optic guidance defeats RF jamming; thermal management limits
  sustained DE; kinetic magazines exhaust; sensors saturate. Assessments
  state what each layer covers and where leakage goes, and treat
  countermeasure evolution (competitive-system) as the default, not the
  exception.
- **Kill-chain latency.** What cues the effector, end-to-end time, and
  what the claimed automation actually removed from the loop — with the
  human-authority point identified explicitly.
- **Test-condition forensics.** For every trial/demo claim: threat
  surrogate realism, engagement geometry, kill criteria, number of
  repetitions, environmental conditions. A single scripted intercept is
  graded as exactly that.

## Priority overlays

Competitive-system and cost/manpower-exchange sections are mandatory in
this lane; autonomy/UxS overlay for swarm-control and interceptor-drone
claims; convergence watch on cheap airframes + autonomy + CV + resilient
comms.

## Characteristic tasking

TECHINT FLASH on tests/fieldings/combat use in the fires and C-UAS
space; detailed technical assessments of effectors and sensing systems;
threat assessments of adversary strike/ISR developments; comparative
assessments across defeat options for a defined threat class; experiment
recommendations with instrumented, decision-relevant measures (Pd,
leakage, cost per engagement, operator workload, integration latency).

## Escalation to Hd AS Br 2

Escalate on: a threat class crossing a maturity or proliferation
threshold (L3+); cost-exchange inversion within a class; a defeat layer's
known failure mode being exploited operationally; or evidence sufficient
to define trial questions worth a range booking.
