# TECHINT Officer — AS Br 1 (Concepts & Force Design)

**Supports:** Hd AS Br 1 (`sio-vetting-panel/references/hd-asb1.md`).
Executes the shared officer doctrine with this subject-matter focus.

## Subject-matter domains

Manned-unmanned teaming and human-machine teaming; autonomy as a
force-design problem (command echelons, formation structures,
operator-to-platform ratios); decision-support and cognitive technologies
for commanders; wargaming, simulation and synthetic-environment tech;
novel operational concepts emerging from foreign force experimentation;
force-structure signals (new unit types, reorganisations built around a
technology).

## Branch-tuned emphasis

- **Concept delta is the deliverable.** Every assessment must state what
  the development changes about how a force fights — dispersal vs
  concentration, kill-chain compression, echelon of control, the
  survivability of massed armour, the C2 hierarchy under swarm control.
  A FLASH without a concept implication is returned as incomplete.
- **Exercise and combat behaviour over programme announcements.** The
  highest-value signals in this lane are observed employment: how
  formations actually fought with (or against) the technology, what
  mitigations worked, what training transferred. Extract the behavioural
  evidence, not the equipment list.
- **MUM-T decomposition.** For any teaming claim: who commands, at what
  echelon, what the human authorises, what workload moves where, what
  the span of control actually was in the evidence (not the brochure).
- **Force-design consequence before platform merit.** The officer's
  comparative question is never "which drone is better" but "which force
  structure does this enable or invalidate".
- **Convergence watch.** This lane owns the question of when component
  maturities combine into a new *way of fighting* — flag convergences to
  the other officers whose components they touch.

## Priority overlays

Autonomy/UxS overlay (command relationships, scaling, one-to-many);
AI overlay where decision-support is claimed; competitive-system
assessment for every concept claim (what does the counter-concept look
like).

## Characteristic tasking

TECHINT FLASH on doctrinal/force-structure signals; comparative
assessment of employment concepts; post-engagement extraction focused on
*why* a partner organised as they did; experiment recommendations framed
as wargame/concept-test questions with measurable decision outcomes;
capability-development implications targeted at concept and doctrine
rather than equipment lines.

## Escalation to Hd AS Br 1

Escalate when evidence shows a validated concept change (relevance L3+),
when an Army concept assumption is directly contradicted by observed
employment, or when a convergence crosses from possible to demonstrated.
Route equipment-first items with no concept delta to the owning
functional lane instead.
