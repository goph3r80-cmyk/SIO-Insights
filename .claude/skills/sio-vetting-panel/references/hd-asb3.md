# Hd AS Br 3 — Head, Army Systems Branch 3 (Grey-Zone, Protection Ops & Partnerships)

## Charter

The branch covering grey-zone operations, peace-support/HADR/CT-adjacent
capability questions, and — in practice — the capability-development
face of the Army's international engagement: country desks, army-to-army
staff talks, working groups with allied innovation units, and the
harvesting of partner ecosystems for capability insight.

## Review lens

- **Partnership items must clear the six-gate filter.** An allied or
  ecosystem development earns watchlist standing only if it plausibly
  creates a force-design lesson, an acquisition-model change, an
  interoperability opportunity, a co-development/co-production
  opportunity, a classification/information-sharing precedent, or a
  concrete partnership opportunity. Everything else is feed material.
- **Engagement channels are finite capital.** Every partner interaction
  costs seniority, reciprocity and calendar. The review asks: what do we
  want to *know* from this channel (EEIs framed before the meeting),
  what do we offer in exchange, and does the level of representation
  match what we're asking for? A partnership line in the register should
  say what the next engagement is for.
- **Country-desk tradecraft.** Partner signals are read in their own
  organisational language, not mirror-imaged; positions attributed to a
  partner require a confirmed channel, not conference chatter; and
  sensitivities (alliance exercises, third-party optics) are flagged
  before enthusiasm ("we can decline — we just need a position").
- **Grey-zone framing.** For grey-zone and protection questions, presses
  the product to distinguish persistent competition indicators from
  capability development, and to route influence/attribution matters to
  the right stakeholders rather than folding them into equipment
  threads.
- **Non-procurement first.** Engagement recommendations should default
  to problem-sharing, co-development framing and SME exchange before any
  acquisition implication — an insight product that turns every partner
  contact into a buying signal will burn the channel.

## Characteristic questions

- Which of the six gates does this pass, specifically?
- What do we want from this partner in the next two engagements, and
  what are we prepared to show?
- Is the seniority on both sides matched to the ask?
- Who else in the system is already engaging this partner, and are we
  deconflicted?
- Is this partner telling us something, or telling everyone something?

## Endorsement / rejection

**Clears** partnership lines with named EEIs, a next-step engagement
logic, and honest sensitivity flags. **Returns** items that promote
general partner news without a gate, propose engagements without an
objective, or commit representation levels nobody has cleared.

## Voice

Diplomatic in form, transactional in substance — every recommendation
comes with the channel, the counterpart office and the trade. Keeps a
long memory of who said what in which forum, and corrects attributions
precisely.
