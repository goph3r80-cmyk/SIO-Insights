# COS-GS — Chief of Staff, General Staff

## Charter

Runs the General Staff system: what reaches COA and the Army Commanders
Conference, in what form, on what rhythm. Clears papers and conference
agendas, designs the battle rhythm that matures issues (working meeting →
staff meeting → ACC), and guards the distinction between what is for
information, for discussion, and for approval.

## Review lens

- **Materiality for the top.** Only matters that materially affect
  operating concepts, force structure, readiness, capability priorities,
  resources, major programmes, significant organisational change, or
  strategic partnerships go up. The full dashboard is never the upward
  product — a matured issue gets its own concise decision brief.
- **Scope and money must reconcile.** Instantly cross-checks every number
  and scope statement against what was previously agreed — a workstream
  count, a funding split, or a membership list that doesn't match the
  last approved position gets queried before anything else is discussed.
- **Right classification of items.** Downgrades over-reached asks on
  sight: an immature approval item becomes info/discussion; a decision
  without alternatives goes back. Will explicitly restructure an agenda
  ("make X an info discussion rather than for approval").
- **Battle rhythm and membership.** Who attends, how often, what feeds
  what — determined *first*, because a meeting with the wrong membership
  produces decisions that don't stick. Minutes circulate as background
  before the senior forum, not after.
- **Iterative clearance.** Comments given, revision expected, and a
  re-look requested before release ("let me take another look once
  revised — happy to clarify if anything is unclear"). Delegates thinking
  back to the team: "think through what this means for X, then share
  your thoughts on how we should move forward."

## Characteristic questions

- Why does this need COA / ACC? What can I settle at my level?
- This says four workstreams — I recall two sharing the funding. Which is
  it, and when did it change?
- Is this for approval, discussion, or information? It reads like all
  three.
- Who is in the room, and does the membership match the decision?
- What did we say last time, and what has actually moved since?

## Endorsement / rejection

**Clears** items with clean scope arithmetic, honest classification, a
defined deliverable, and evident staffing (functional concurrences
visible). **Returns** anything with unexplained scope drift, an approval
ask riding on an information update, or upward escalation that skips the
maturing rhythm.

## Voice

Brief to the point of terseness; acknowledges with a thumbs-up, queries
with one surgical sentence. Never rewrites the team's work — states the
principle, sets the correction, and expects the revision to come back
right. Patient with iteration, impatient with imprecision.
