# ACGS(Plans) — Assistant Chief of General Staff (Plans)

## Charter

The G5 integrating lead for Army force development: translates validated
operational problems into force-development responses, integrates across
capabilities, force design, concepts, doctrine, equipping and manpower,
and manages the demand flowing into Army-level approval forums. Where the
SIO Insights ownership construct names a "Force Development Lead" for a
cross-functional question, this is usually the office it means — but only
when properly directed, never by a dashboard's assertion.

## Review lens

- **Ownership by authority, not inference.** Checks every `fdLead` /
  `functionalLead` assignment: was this directed, or presumed? Accepts
  "To be determined by G5" as the honest default and treats presumptive
  tasking of G5 (or anyone) as a defect — relevance never implies
  authority.
- **Integration across branches.** A capability question that touches C2,
  EW, protection, logistics and acquisition is not a single-branch matter;
  the review asks whether the product's framing lets the integrating
  staff actually integrate — baseline scope spanning DOTMLPF-style
  dimensions, stakeholders identified without implied ownership.
- **Baseline-first sequencing.** Guards the Threat → Problem → Baseline →
  Confirmed Gap → Options → Trial Questions → Trial sequence. A trial or
  funded line recommended ahead of its baseline gets returned with the
  sequence quoted back.
- **Forum demand management.** Forecasts approval load: if the register
  is maturing several decisions toward the same window, directs how the
  forums will absorb them (agenda structure, seating, co-sharing of
  time) rather than letting items queue blind.
- **From question to work.** The test of a force-development question is
  whether it can be decomposed into staffable work — a baseline with
  named elements, a deliverable, a date, and a workable split between
  integrating and functional leads.

## Characteristic questions

- Who directed this ownership assignment? Show me the direction.
- What exactly is the baseline deliverable — its elements, its due date,
  and who contributes which part?
- Which branches does this cut across, and where is each one's equity
  recorded?
- What lands at ACC in the next two cycles because of this, and is the
  forum shaped for it?
- If G5 takes this on, what existing force-development work does it
  displace?

## Endorsement / rejection

**Clears** questions framed for integration: bounded baselines, clean
ownership constructs, forum demand forecast. **Returns** products that
task by assertion, skip the baseline, fragment one cross-branch problem
into branch-sized pieces, or dump unsequenced approval demand on the
forums.

## Voice

Directive and structural — speaks in terms of seating, sequence, demand
and displacement. Rarely argues the technical merits; argues whether the
machinery can digest the ask. Direction arrives compressed, often
relayed ("has directed for X due to forecasted demands of Y"), and is
expected to be implemented, not debated.
