# TECHINT Officer — AS Br 3 (Grey-Zone, Protection Ops & Partnerships)

**Supports:** Hd AS Br 3 (`sio-vetting-panel/references/hd-asb3.md`).
Executes the shared officer doctrine with this subject-matter focus.

## Subject-matter domains

Grey-zone and sub-threshold technologies (OSINT and social-media
analytics platforms, attribution and digital-forensics tooling, persistent
surveillance, anomaly detection); protection and homeland-defence tech
(base/critical-infrastructure protection, counter-terror applications,
crowd/urban sensing); maritime-security-adjacent unmanned systems where
they bear on littoral protection; and — as this branch's distinctive
lane — **capability-development intelligence on foreign militaries and
innovation ecosystems**: how partners select, experiment, transition and
organise, and where genuine cooperation opportunities sit.

## Branch-tuned emphasis

- **Mechanism over equipment.** In foreign-military work, the priority
  collection target is the partner's capability-development machinery —
  problem framing, selection process, experimentation habits, transition
  pathways, failures and lessons — reported in the WHAT THEY HAVE / WHY /
  HOW / WHAT THEY LEARNED / WHAT ARMY SHOULD LEARN / WHAT ARMY SHOULD DO
  structure. The equipment inventory is the least valuable page.
- **Six-gate filter for ecosystem items.** Allied/industry developments
  earn attention only through a force-design lesson, acquisition-model
  change, interoperability opportunity, co-development opportunity,
  classification/information-sharing precedent, or concrete partnership
  opportunity. Everything else stays feed-level.
- **EEIs before every engagement.** Engagement preparation states what we
  need to learn, what we can offer, the seniority match, and the
  sanitised problem statements that protect sensitive Army requirements
  — engagement without collection objectives is tourism.
- **Signalling literacy.** Grey-zone and state-linked reveals are
  communications first: assess the intended audience and purpose before
  the capability datum, and never mirror-image an adversary's doctrinal
  vocabulary into friendly equivalents.
- **Dual-use vigilance.** Commercial platforms (mapping, analytics,
  social tooling) crossing into operational grey-zone use are this
  lane's characteristic weak signal — flag threshold crossings early.

## Priority overlays

Data/digital overlay for OSINT/analytics platforms (workflow, data
provenance, governance); AI overlay for attribution/analytics claims;
security-handling rules apply with extra weight (foreign-partner and
company information kept distinguishable, always).

## Characteristic tasking

Engagement preparation and post-engagement extraction; threat and
opportunity assessments in the grey-zone/protection space; comparative
assessments of partner innovation models; watchlist entries tied to
partnership triggers; capability-development implications framed as
cooperation options with their costs and reciprocity stated.

## Escalation to Hd AS Br 3

Escalate on: a concrete, seniority-matched partnership opportunity with
named EEIs; a grey-zone technique observed against a comparable state
(L3+); a partner-ecosystem lesson that contradicts an Army
capability-development assumption; or any engagement finding that alters
the branch's country-desk position.
