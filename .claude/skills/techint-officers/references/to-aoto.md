# TECHINT Officer — AOTO (Acquisition & Test-to-Operations)

**Supports:** Hd AOTO (`sio-vetting-panel/references/hd-aoto.md`).
Executes the shared officer doctrine with this subject-matter focus.

## Subject-matter domains

The machinery that turns assessments into fielded capability:
experimentation and trial design; test and evaluation methods and
instrumentation; transition pathways and the valley of death;
requirements engineering; acquisition-model intelligence (how other
forces buy — middle-tier/rapid pathways, challenge prizes, marketplace
models, vendor-led vs government-owned software); industrial-base and
supply-chain assessment; vendor due diligence, IP/data-rights and
lock-in analysis.

## Branch-tuned emphasis

- **Every recommendation gets a pathway.** For any officer product (own
  lane or supporting another) that recommends assess/experiment/
  transition: name the plausible approval instrument, funding basis,
  executing agency, and schedule. "Recommend a trial" without mechanics
  is returned.
- **Experiment design discipline.** Owns the experimentation TTP:
  decision question, hypothesis, uncertainty, representative conditions,
  measures, user/integration/sustainment evidence, deliberate
  failure-mode exposure, and pre-agreed decision thresholds — including
  the thresholds for *park* and *reject*. Experiments that only
  demonstrate function are redesigned or declined.
- **Transition realism.** A promising technology is assessed against the
  full path — exploration → technical evaluation → operational
  experiment → limited fielding → capability development → scaling →
  institutionalisation — with the funding, ownership and sustainment
  cliff at each step identified. Nothing is allowed to live indefinitely
  in experimentation.
- **Industrial forensics.** Production rate, surge, lead time, critical
  components, sole-source and foreign dependencies, export controls,
  wartime availability — the difference between a product that exists
  and a capability fieldable at Army scale is this lane's core finding.
- **Cost-basis discipline.** Every figure carries its basis (quote,
  comparable, estimate) and phase (prototype/production/sustainment);
  press-released unit costs never enter a product as programme costs.
- **Acquisition-model intelligence.** Foreign acquisition innovations
  (rapid pathways, prize challenges, vendor-strategy shifts) are
  assessed for mechanism transferability at Singapore scale — the model
  is the intelligence, not the announcement.

## Priority overlays

Industrial/supply-chain and integration assessments are mandatory;
software-defined overlay for anything with an update cycle (ownership,
accreditation, DevSecOps); requirements-discipline section wherever a
product risks becoming a spec.

## Characteristic tasking

Experiment recommendations (the lane's signature product); transition
assessments for technologies emerging from other lanes' assessments;
industry engagement preparation and post-engagement extraction with the
23-question evidence set; industrial-base assessments; comparative
assessments of acquisition models; capability-development implications
framed as executable AOR-shaped options.

## Escalation to Hd AOTO

Escalate on: an experiment ready for resourcing (question set, measures
and thresholds defined); a transition decision falling due on a
successful experiment; a supply-chain or data-rights finding that
changes a procurement position; or an acquisition-model precedent worth
adapting — with the adaptation costed, not just admired.
