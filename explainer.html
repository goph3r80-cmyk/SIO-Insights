Explainer · v3.0

An Army decision-support system, organised around persistent questions

SIO Insights is no longer a news digest. Its organising principle is the Army Question Register — six to ten persistent force-development questions the Army must answer. External news remains an input; every incoming signal is graded, assessed by a branch TECHINT lane, mapped against a registered question, and only escalated when it materially changes something. The fundamental test applied to every development: does this change what the Army can, should or must do? The standing intent lives in DOCTRINE.md; everything below is how the live system executes it.

1

The operating loop

Question → Data → Insight → Decision → Action → Effect → Updated Assessment
INTAKE

Trawl & dragnet

A tiered sweep of trade press, think tanks, wires and regional desks (TRAWLERPROMPT.md), plus a Google Alerts keyword dragnet ingested in code (scripts/ingest-alerts.js): canonical URLs resolved, duplicates removed, hits pre-tagged by question.

Output: deduped, sourced candidates
→
GRADE

TECHINT assessment

Every candidate is claim-decomposed and graded — source vs claim, corroboration vs syndication, honest maturity placement, the material-change test (techint-assessment skill). State-media and vendor claims are capped, never laundered into fact.

Output: graded evidence
→
ASSESS

Branch TECHINT officers

Six branch-tuned analysts (techint-officers skill) answer what changed · why now · so what · so what for Army, place items on the L0–L7 relevance ladder, and recommend from the full range — ignore to elevate, never defaulting to "experiment" or "buy".

Output: branch assessments
→
REGISTER

Questions & threads

The Generator (GENERATORPROMPT.md) folds assessed evidence into data.js: the question register, decision threads, typed senior direction, decision history — with quiet questions honestly marked unchanged.

Output: window.SIO_DATA v3
→
VET

Panel, publish, loop

The nine-appointment vetting panel (sio-vetting-panel skill) interrogates the product before it ships via PR review to the dashboard. Direction given is logged, and outcomes flow back as the next edition's evidence.

Output: vetted edition + decision log
2

Three collections, three altitudes

questions[] → serials[] (threads) → feed[]

Questions — the register

6–10 persistent Army questions (Q1–Q7…)

Each carries the enduring question, its decision horizon, the Army baseline (authorised internal information only — honestly "not established" until it is), the external change, the delta (which Army assumption may need reassessment), split evidence/inference confidence, typed escalation triggers, any direction required, and a decision log that persists after the paper is delivered. Threads and evidence attach to questions — never the other way round.

Q1 — Affordable Layered C-UAS
How is the changing UAS threat altering the protection architecture, cost exchange and magazine depth required by Army?

Threads & feed — the working layers

decision threads over a provenance feed

Serials are decision threads attached to a question: the recommendation, options, baseline deliverable, four-role ownership and evidence for one strand of it. The feed is the evidence base — every kept story with source type, corroboration, confidence and canonical URL. It sits below the decision layer for provenance and analyst use; its size is deliberately not a headline metric.

The analysis logic

what changed → why now → so what → so what for Army → gaps → action

Every assessment must progress past "Country X demonstrated System Y": what threshold actually moved (cost, maturity, scale, combat validation), why it is consequential now, what changes in operations, which Army assumption or capability is affected, what evidence is missing, and what Army should do next. Here's a thread card from a recent edition, annotated.

W-1 THREAD mixed (reported – combat-proven) P5 US Robotics
Maritime autonomy — attritable USV strike & sensing
Hold at watchlist — no Army capability ownership assigned pending a defined littoral requirement.
Signal (graded)
[Combat-proven] First US combat use of lethal USVs — three armed one-way-attack craft struck a naval base, run outside the standing unmanned-systems task-force framework. [Reported] A production-track attritable interceptor USV completed government-led autonomy and degraded-environment assessments. Each claim carries its own maturity tag and provenance.
Army relevance
Stated plainly: indirect — no Army littoral requirement has been identified; current value is the command-authority precedent feeding the human-authority question (Q6). No relevance is asserted that the evidence doesn't support.
Escalation trigger
Escalate only on a defined Army littoral/riverine requirement, an identified use case, a specific participation mechanism, or a force-development sponsor — not because another reporting cycle arrived.
Sponsor SIO FD lead Unassigned — pending an Army requirement Question Q6
Reading a thread card (v3 fields)
Signal
The graded evidence — every claim tagged inline with its maturity and provenance ([Fielded], [Reported — state-linked, corroborate]), organised by threat class or operational effect, never by press release.
Recommendation / Why now / Army problem
The decision brief: what is asked, what changed, and the problem stated without inferring internal Army facts — "establish whether existing and planned capabilities adequately address…", never "Army has no X" from open sources.
Options & direction sought
Genuine alternatives with trade-offs, and the typed ask — a decision without alternatives goes back.
Watch fields
Watch threads carry latest development, change from previous ("unchanged" is a valid, honest answer), Army relevance (stated plainly when not yet established) and an escalation trigger — escalation is event-driven, never calendar-driven.
Ownership
Four roles, never one vague owner: Sponsor (usually SIO — keeps the question and evidence), FD lead (G5 where directed; honestly "unassigned" or "TBD by G5" otherwise), Functional lead, Supporting. Relevance never implies authority.
Question link
Every thread names the register question it serves. An item that materially affects no question stays in the feed — technological interest is not a promotion criterion.

4a

The discipline rules

what keeps this decision-grade
Non-negotiables (DOCTRINE.md)
Evidence wall
Open sources can establish external threats, maturity and trends — never an internal Army gap, baseline, cost or owner. The chain is external signal → possible implication → internal baseline → confirmed gap.
Baseline before trial
Threat → problem → baseline → confirmed gap → options → trial questions → trial. No jumping from foreign demos to funded programmes.
Material-change test
An update surfaces only if it changed the threat, maturity, affordability, an assumption, a gap, an opportunity, a course, or a trigger. Otherwise the evidence base updates silently.
Honest maturity & confidence
The ladder — reported → demonstrated → operationally evaluated → fielded → combat-proven — applied per claim; one test is never "proven", a parade is never "fielded", and prose never outruns the confidence grade.
Closed loop
Direction given → responsible staff → deliverable → result → effect → updated assessment. The register is institutional memory for why decisions were made and what happened after.
Decision-advantage metrics
Value is measured in provenance coverage, internal-data coverage and closed-loop rate — never article count.
Information-and-action ladder
DCM/ACDOM sit in the spine: every lifecycle stage has a decision instrument, and that instrument has information requirements. An assessment states which requirements its evidence satisfies, which are open, and whose action closes each gap — or it is analysis, not decision support.

4c

The landing zone

where register items enter the real staff system — DCM & ACDOM

DCM — the lifecycle

The Defence Capability Management framework is the MINDEF lifecycle process by which a capability need becomes operational and support-system requirements and is managed cradle to retirement: long-term planning → front-end planning → acquisition management → transition to O&S → O&S → retirement. SIO conducts DCM of Army systems and platforms in the system-development lane. Every decision-tier thread now carries a dcmPhase (the Army thread's state, from evidenced internal facts only — never inferred from a foreign system's maturity), a nextInstrument (the staffing document that would move it: OCF, OMP, EMP, AOR/SON/SOR, AOE, PIP…), and a targetForum.

ACDOM — the governance

The Army Capability Development Operating Manual defines three governance tiers: executive-level working committees (PEO-anchored, with a CSIO–Dir(Land) system-integration forum, WSSC staffing and the equipping management forum); General Staff forums — ACDM (monthly, chaired COS-GS, the pinnacle cap-dev forum), ASTC (CSIO co-chaired R&T gate), CPRC, AOM, ADTC; and Army-level steering committees — ASSC, AISC, ACC. Direction types route accordingly: priority directions and capability decisions to ACDM (R&T gating at ASTC), resource decisions to CPRC/ACDM, policy-doctrine consequences to ADTC/AOM. Each edition nominates at most one or two material changes as ACDM agenda candidates, in decision form; the register records candidacy and evidenced outcomes, never attendance. See the capdev-governance skill.

A choice between capability solutions or architectures.

resource_decision

Funding, manpower or programme resources committed.

policy_doctrine_decision

Policy, authority, doctrine or governance determined.

staff_action

No decision required — tasked within existing authority.

maturity — the ladder, applied per claim

reported

Announced, revealed, solicited or paraded — existence, not capability.

demonstrated

A controlled test with an observable result. One test is never "proven".

operationally_evaluated

Evaluated with operational units under operational conditions.

fielded

Issued to operating units — a production contract alone doesn't qualify.

combat_proven

Verified combat employment — for that event's conditions only.

confidence & status

high / moderate / low

Claim-level confidence; state-media and uncorroborated vendor claims cap at low. Questions carry it split: evidence vs inference.

not_started · in_progress

Where the tasked work stands.

overdue

A suspense passed without a delivered output — flagged honestly, never silently rolled.

completed · closed

Delivered, or retired with its decision history preserved.
